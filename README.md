## 🛠️ Step 1: Understanding GitHub & GitHub Pages

* **GitHub** is a cloud service that securely stores your code and project history.
* **GitHub Pages** is a free hosting feature that turns any public repository into a fast, live website with free SSL (`https://`).
* Special rule: If your GitHub username is `jayeshkhatwani`, naming your repository `jayeshkhatwani.github.io` makes your website instantly live at:

## 🚀 Step 2: How to Deploy to GitHub Pages (2 Easy Methods)

### Method A: Web Browser (Drag & Drop — No Software Needed!)

1. Go to [GitHub.com](https://github.com) and log in (or sign up for free).
2. Click the **`+`** icon at the top right ➔ click **New repository**.
3. **Repository name**: Type `jayeshkhatwani.github.io` *(replace with your exact GitHub username)*.
4. Set the repository to **Public** ➔ Check **"Add a README file"** ➔ Click **Create repository**.
5. In your new repository, click **Add file** ➔ **Upload files**.
6. Drag and drop all the files from this folder (`index.html`, `styles.css`, `app.js`, `sitemap.xml`, `robots.txt`) into GitHub.
7. Click **Commit changes**.
8. Go to **Settings** (tab at the top) ➔ click **Pages** (on the left menu).
9. Under **Branch**, select `main` (or `master`) and `/root` ➔ click **Save**.
10. 🎉 **Done!** Within 60 seconds, your site will be live at `https://<your-username>.github.io`!

## 🔍 Step 3: How to Appear on Google Search (SEO & Google Search Console)

To ensure that searching *"Jayesh Khatwani"*, *"Jayesh Khatwani Aerospace"*, or *"Jayesh Khatwani SRM"* brings your website directly to the top of Google:

### 1. Pre-configured SEO in this Website:
* **JSON-LD Schema**: Embedded `Person` markup telling Google your name, university (SRM), alumni, and skills.
* **OpenGraph Tags**: When sharing your link on **LinkedIn**, **Twitter/X**, or **WhatsApp**, it automatically generates a sleek aerospace preview card.
* **Sitemap & Robots**: Pre-configured `sitemap.xml` and `robots.txt` for search crawlers.

### 2. Submit to Google Search Console (Takes 2 Minutes):
1. Go to [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console).
2. Click **Start Now** and log in with your Google account (`jk032004de@gmail.com`).
3. Under **URL prefix**, enter your website URL: `https://<your-username>.github.io/` ➔ Click **Continue**.
4. Choose **HTML tag** verification method ➔ copy the meta tag code (e.g. `<meta name="google-site-verification" content="..." />`) and paste it into the `<head>` of your `index.html`.
5. In Google Search Console, go to **Sitemaps** on the left menu.
6. Enter `sitemap.xml` and click **Submit**.
7. Click **URL Inspection**, paste your homepage URL, and click **"Request Indexing"**.

Google will crawl and list your website in search results within 2 to 7 days!
